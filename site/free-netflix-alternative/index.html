A free Netflix alternative for Windows

Velmora isn't a streaming service with its own library. It's a free, open-source app that searches many sources at once, picks the best link and plays it in a built-in 4K player. No subscription, no card, one free account.

Download for Windows See what it does

Free. Windows 10 and 11. Android, iPhone, Mac and Linux are coming soon.

What Velmora is, and what it isn't

You type a movie, series or anime title. Velmora checks many third-party sources at the same time, ranks the links it finds by quality, codec, language and size, and starts the best one. If a link fails, the next source starts at the same second.

It doesn't host, upload or store any content, and it has no catalogue of originals. What you can watch depends on what those sources offer, and you're responsible for what you watch and for following the law where you live.

Why people use it

  • One search, every source. The same title found on different sites shows up once.
  • The best link, picked for you. It's ready by the time you press Play.
  • Pick up where you stopped. Continue Watching remembers the exact second and starts the next episode.
  • Theatre. Scheduled screenings everyone watches at the same moment, with a live chat that is never saved.
  • Sync. AniList and Trakt mark what you finish as watched on their own.
  • Open and checkable. The source is public, and GitHub Actions builds every installer from it.

Questions

Is Velmora really free?

Yes. The full app is free and open source under GPL-3.0, with no subscription and no card. Velmora Public shows a few quiet ads, never while a video plays. Velmora Max is the same app with no ads and is given out by the developer.

Does Velmora replace Netflix?

Not exactly. Netflix has its own licensed catalogue and original shows. Velmora has no library of its own: it searches third-party sources for the title you ask for and plays the best link it finds. What you can watch depends on those sources.

Is Velmora legal to use?

Velmora doesn't host, upload or store any content. It searches third-party sites the way a browser does and plays what they serve. You're responsible for what you watch and for following the law where you live.

Does it work on Mac, Linux, iPhone, Android or a TV?

Not yet. Velmora is Windows only for now. Android (a full build designed with Material You), iPhone, iPad and Mac (native SwiftUI apps with Liquid Glass) and Linux builds are coming soon.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

Velmora isn't code-signed yet, because signing certificates are expensive. Windows SmartScreen warns about any unsigned app it hasn't seen often. The source is public and GitHub Actions builds every installer from it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a free one, made once in your browser with Google, Discord or an emailed code. It keeps your list, progress and settings in step across PCs. Only that first sign-in needs the internet; after it, Velmora opens offline too.
